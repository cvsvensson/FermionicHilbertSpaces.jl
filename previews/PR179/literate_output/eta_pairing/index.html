η-pairing in a large Hubbard chain

Symbolic kets store only their nonzero amplitudes and operators act on them without a basis, so exact calculations are possible in spaces far too large for vectors. Here we build Yang's η-pairing eigenstates[1] of a 200-site Hubbard chain and show that they have off-diagonal long-range order: their pair correlations do not decay with distance.

η-pairing states

The η-pairing operator $η^\dagger = \sum_j (-1)^j c_{j\uparrow}^\dagger c_{j\downarrow}^\dagger$ creates a doubly occupied site with a staggered phase, which makes it commute with the hopping on a bipartite lattice. The states $ψ_n = (η^\dagger)^n|0⟩$ are then eigenstates of the Hubbard model with energy $n(U - 2μ)$. Each is an equal-weight sum over the $\binom{L}{n}$ ways to place $n$ doublons, so the pair correlation

\[⟨c_{i\uparrow}^\dagger c_{i\downarrow}^\dagger c_{j\downarrow} c_{j\uparrow}⟩ = (-1)^{i+j} \frac{n(L-n)}{L(L-1)}\]

is the same for every pair of distinct sites.

A 200-site chain

The Hilbert space has dimension $4^L = 2^{2L}$, while $ψ_n$ is a sum of only $\binom{L}{n}$ basis states.

using FermionicHilbertSpaces, LinearAlgebrausing FermionicHilbertSpaces.NonCommutativeProducts: nterms@fermions cL = 200H = hilbert_space(c, [(j, s) for j in 1:L for s in (:↑, :↓)])t, U, μ = 1.0, 4.0, 0.5n(j, s) = c[j, s]' * c[j, s]ham = sum(-t * c[j, s]' * c[j + 1, s] + hc for j in 1:(L - 1) for s in (:↑, :↓)) +    sum(U * n(j, :↑) * n(j, :↓) - μ * (n(j, :↑) + n(j, :↓)) for j in 1:L)η = sum((-1)^j * c[j, :↑]' * c[j, :↓]' for j in 1:L)ψs = accumulate((ψ, _) -> η * ψ, 1:2; init = Ket("0"^(2L), H))for (N, ψ) in enumerate(ψs)    @assert nterms(ψ) == binomial(L, N)    @assert norm(ham * ψ - N * (U - 2μ) * ψ) < 1.0e-10end(log2_dimension = log2(dim(H)), terms_in_ψ₂ = nterms(ψs[2]))
(log2_dimension = 400.0, terms_in_ψ₂ = 19900)

The pair correlation of $ψ_2$, here from site 1 to every other site, is the same at all distances.
