η-pairing in a large Hubbard chain

Symbolic kets live in a Hilbert space without enumerating its basis, so they can be used where the full space is far too large for vectors. Here we build exact eigenstates of a Hubbard chain with L = 100 spinful sites, a Hilbert space of dimension 4^100.

using FermionicHilbertSpaces, LinearAlgebra@fermions cL = 100H = hilbert_space(c, [(j, s) for j in 1:L for s in (:↑, :↓)])dim(H)

The η-pairing operator creates a doubly occupied site with a staggered phase. The phase makes η† commute with the hopping on a bipartite lattice, so the states ψₙ = (η†)ⁿ|0⟩ are eigenstates of H with energy Eₙ = n(U - 2μ).

η = sum((-1)^j * c[j, :↑]' * c[j, :↓]' for j in 1:L)
Sum with 100 terms: 
-c†[1, :↑]*c†[1, :↓]-c†[11, :↑]*c†[11, :↓]-c†[13, :↑]*c†[13, :↓] + ...

Starting from the vacuum, we apply η† repeatedly. ψₙ is a sparse sum over the binomial(L, n) ways of placing n doublons.

ψs = [Kets(H)("0"^(2L)) + 0]for _ in 1:2    push!(ψs, η * ψs[end])end

H acts term by term on each symbolic state, and we compare with the expected energy.

for (k, ψ) in enumerate(ψs)    N = k - 1    E = N * (U - 2μ)    residual = norm(sum(h * ψ for h in hterms) - E * ψ)    println("n = $N: E = $E, basis states = $(length(ψ.dict)) = $(binomial(L, N)), residual = $residual")end
n = 0: E = 0.0, basis states = 1 = 1, residual = 0.0
n = 1: E = 3.0, basis states = 100 = 100, residual = 0.0
n = 2: E = 6.0, basis states = 4950 = 4950, residual = 0.0

The residuals vanish exactly: the states form an equally spaced tower inside a space of 2^200 states. See C. N. Yang, Phys. Rev. Lett. 63, 2144 (1989) and Moudgalya, Regnault, Bernevig, arXiv:2004.13727.
